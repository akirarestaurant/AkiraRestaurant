About us

Welcome to Akirá.

Who we are, how the community works and how to get involved.

On this page

    Welcome

    Akirá Restaurant is a friendly Discord community built around a shared love of restaurant-style roleplay and good company. Whether you are here to chat, to take part in events or to help run the community, there is a seat at the table for you.

    This page explains how the community is organised, what we expect from our members and how you can become more involved over time.

    The community

    Our server is open to everyone and free to join. Members gather in text and voice channels to talk, play and take part in seasonal activities. This season, the server is celebrating Halloween.

    💬

    Conversation

    Text and voice channels for everyday chat.

    🍽️

    Roleplay

    Restaurant scenarios for guests and staff alike.

    🎉

    Events

    Seasonal and themed activities for members.

    👀

    Previews

    Development leaks shared with our members.

    Community guidelines

    We keep the community welcoming by asking every member to follow a few simple principles. Select a guideline to read more.

    Treat everyone with respect

    Be kind to staff and guests alike. Disagreements should be handled calmly, and harassment of any kind is not tolerated.

    Keep conversations friendly and on topic

    Use each channel for its intended purpose so that everyone can find what they are looking for.

    Follow Discord's rules

    All members must abide by Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, as well as the rules posted in the server.

    Listen to the team

    If a member of staff asks you to change something, please cooperate. Concerns can be raised respectfully through a ticket.

          Staff conduct

          Keep the password confidential

          Your staff password is for staff only. Never share it with members, and tell an owner immediately if you think it has been shared.

          Act professionally

          You represent Akirá. Be courteous, fair and consistent with every member, and keep your replies clear and respectful.

          Escalate when unsure

          If a situation is beyond your role, contact an owner rather than guessing. It is always fine to ask.

          Stay active

          Activity and engagement are how staff earn and keep their position. Let an owner know in advance if you will be away.

          Access and security

          Passwords are issued by an owner. If you need access, or a password stops working, contact an owner. Always sign out when using a shared device.
